# 🎬 Anime & Cartoon AI Video Studio (100% Free)
### Complete Text-to-Video Production Studio for Faceless YouTube Channels (Shorts & Longform)

A high-performance, **100% Free / Zero-Cost** automated AI video creation platform built with Python. Generates ready-to-upload YouTube anime and cartoon videos from text prompts and story ideas without requiring paid APIs, subscriptions, or expensive GPU hardware.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Sound Design

- **🚀 1-Click YouTube Faceless Video Creator**: Enter any topic (e.g. *"Weakest hunter awakens shadow sovereign powers"*) and generate a complete video with script, voiceover, anime art, camera motion, sound effects, background music, and animated subtitles.
- **🎵 Attractive Studio Anime OST Background Music**:
  - `Epic Anime Battle` (Driving 130 BPM brass chords, 808 sub-bass, and marching percussion)
  - `Emotional & Wholesome (Ghibli vibe)` (Lush Major 7th piano chords & music box melody)
  - `Chill Lofi Anime Beats` (Warm Rhodes electric piano, vinyl crackle & boom bap drums)
  - `Suspense & Dark Fantasy` (Dissonant drones & deep sub pulses)
  - `Upbeat & Adventurous` (High energy anime opening synth beats)
- **💥 High-Impact Anime Sound Effects (SFX)**:
  - `whoosh` (Cinematic swept air slice)
  - `sword_slash` (Sharp metallic katana unsheath & blade clash)
  - `dramatic_boom` (Massive Hollywood 808 sub-bass drop)
  - `thunder_strike` (Lightning crack + rolling thunder reverb)
  - `magic_chime` (Crystalline anime sparkle bell tree)
  - `laser_energy` (Sci-fi energy beam blast)
  - `dragon_roar` (Deep monster roar & sub bass)
  - `heartbeat_suspense` (Dramatic chest-thumping tension thuds)
- **📤 Upload Your Own Custom Music & SFX**: Drag and drop any custom MP3 or WAV file into the studio to use your own favorite anime OSTs or trending audio!
- **🎙️ Zero-Cost Neural Voiceover**: Microsoft Edge-TTS neural speech with character voices (Epic Narrator, Anime Hero, Ghibli Storyteller, Cute Anime Girl, Japanese Subbed Style, Hindi Narrator) with automatic BGM ducking.
- **🎬 Dynamic Multi-Layer Video FX**: Floating anime energy particles, embers, and action speedlines in 1080p Full HD (8 Mbps H.264).
- **✨ Animated Karaoke Subtitles**: High-retention viral YouTube Shorts / TikTok style bold typography with black border and drop shadows rendered directly onto video frames.
- **🎬 Storyboard & Scene Director**: Full visual editor to inspect, customize, and fine-tune individual scene scripts, prompts, camera motion, and sound effects before final export.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### 1. Run the Web Studio
Open your terminal inside the project directory:
```bash
python app.py
```

### 2. Open in Browser
Navigate to:
```
http://127.0.0.1:7860
```

### 3. Audio & Sound Design Studio
Go to the **🎙️ Audio & Sound Design Studio** tab to audition any BGM track, listen to every sound effect, or upload your own MP3 tracks!
